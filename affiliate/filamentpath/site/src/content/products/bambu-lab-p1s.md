The Bambu Lab P1S is the segment's reference for enclosed CoreXY printing under $700. Compared to the A1 series (open-frame bed-slingers), the P1S adds an enclosure and CoreXY motion system, which together change what materials are realistically printable: ABS and ASA become viable, and acceleration limits move higher.

The enclosure is passive (no active heating beyond what the bed contributes), so for materials that need a hotter chamber than the bed alone provides — polycarbonate, nylon — the P1S is still a compromise. For the typical hobbyist workflow of PLA + PETG + occasional ABS, it covers the entire common case.

Versus the A1 at $300 less, the P1S earns its premium primarily on two axes: materials range (enclosed ABS without modding) and motion system (CoreXY scales acceleration better than bed-slinger). The AMS integration is identical to the A1 family.
