Sunlu PETG is the entry-tier PETG of choice for cost-conscious users who don't want to commit to premium brands until they've tuned their workflow. The vacuum-sealed packaging is a real practical win — PETG absorbs moisture quickly off the spool, and starting with a dry roll matters for first-print quality.

The spec sheet (230-250°C nozzle, 70-80°C bed) lines up with typical PETG profiles and works across most consumer FDM printers without modification. Where Sunlu sometimes disappoints is consistency: long-running Reddit threads document occasional bad batches with diameter drift or inconsistent gloss, particularly on translucent colors. For learning the material, the price-to-quality ratio is sound; for production-grade work, premium brands (Polymaker PolyLite PETG, Prusament PETG) have tighter QC.

Workflow note: PETG strings more than PLA. Default slicer profiles tuned for PLA will produce visible stringing on any PETG, Sunlu or otherwise. Plan to dial in retraction settings.
