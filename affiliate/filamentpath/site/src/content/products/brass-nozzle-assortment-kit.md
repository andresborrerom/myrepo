A brass nozzle assortment kit is one of the highest leverage cheap upgrades a new FDM user can make. The default 0.4mm nozzle that ships with most printers is a good general-purpose pick, but specific use cases want specific sizes: 0.2mm for fine miniatures, 0.6mm for fast functional parts, 0.8mm for bulk prototyping where layer time matters more than detail. A multi-size kit at $18 is roughly the cost of two single OEM nozzles.

Brass is the correct material for the common case: PLA, PETG, ABS, TPU, ASA — all non-abrasive. The moment a user switches to anything filled (carbon fiber, glass fiber, glow-in-dark, metal-filled, glitter), brass wears in tens of hours. For abrasive filaments, hardened steel or ruby-tipped nozzles are the right purchase; this kit is the wrong tool for that job and should not be sold as such.

Compatibility caveat that catches new buyers: the MK8 form factor is *not* the same as Bambu Lab's quick-swap hotend system. A Bambu A1 / P1S owner cannot use this kit — they need Bambu's own (or hot-end-compatible) nozzles. Buyers should confirm their printer's hotend revision before ordering.
