A generic PEI textured build plate is the most-common first accessory upgrade for Bambu A1 / A1 mini owners. The OEM plate is solid but expensive; aftermarket equivalents at one-third the price are widely available and, in most cases, indistinguishable in daily use. The differentiator is per-seller QC — some Amazon listings have visibly flatter substrates than others, so reading recent verified-purchase reviews matters more than for OEM parts.

Functionally, a textured PEI surface gives the bottom of every print a uniform matte texture, which most users find visually preferable to glossy smooth-PEI marks. The spring-steel construction lets the user pop the plate off and flex it slightly to release stuck parts, which is meaningfully easier than scraping a fixed-bed setup.

Caveats: PEI is a thermoplastic, and prolonged ABS or ASA printing on the same spot can degrade the texture and adhesion over time. Heavy multi-material printers benefit from rotating between plates. PETG-specific note: even on textured PEI, tall PETG prints occasionally need a thin glue-stick layer to prevent adhesion *to* the plate from being too strong (counterintuitive — PETG can fuse to PEI and chip out a chunk of plate on removal).
