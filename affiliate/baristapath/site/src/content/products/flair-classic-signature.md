The Flair Classic Signature is the entry point into manual lever espresso. At a fraction of the cost of the Flair 58 or Cafelat Robot, it delivers the same fundamental concept: lever pressure produces 9-bar extraction, no electricity required, packs into a case for travel.

The compromise versus the 58 is the portafilter. The Classic uses a proprietary smaller-format brewing chamber rather than a 58mm commercial portafilter. That limits aftermarket options and caps dose size around 18 grams — fine for traditional Italian-style shots, restrictive for modern light-roast triple ristrettos.

For under $150, it's a real espresso machine that produces real espresso. Many home baristas start here and either fall in love with the lever workflow (and upgrade to a Flair 58) or move horizontally to a semi-automatic. Either way, the Classic is one of the cheapest legitimate ways into the hobby.
