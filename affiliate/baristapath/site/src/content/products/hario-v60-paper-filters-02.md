The Hario V60 Paper Filter Size 02 is the OEM filter for the Hario V60 02 dripper — same brand, designed-for-dripper fit, and the filter the Hoffmann and Kasuya V60 recipes were calibrated against. For the V60, OEM filters are the safe default; off-brand "V60-compatible" filters work but introduce a small risk of poor seating that lets water bypass the bed.

The 40-count pack is the sample-size SKU; for households brewing once a day, the 100-count or 200-count packs are better value per filter. The bleached white version (VCF-02-40W) is what we recommend by default — modern oxygen bleaching is chlorine-free and produces less paper taste with a brief 5-second rinse than the natural (unbleached) variant. The natural version needs 15-20 seconds of hot-water rinsing to fully strip the paper note; the bleached version is closer to neutral straight out of the pack.

Filter choice will not save a bad brew, and no V60 filter on the market will ruin a good one. The reason to pick OEM Hario over generic is predictability — the exact fit removes one variable from the brewing setup, leaves you to focus on grind and pour technique, and costs roughly $0.18 per cup. Worth it for the simplicity.
