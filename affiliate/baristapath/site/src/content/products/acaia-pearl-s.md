The Acaia Pearl S is the specialty-coffee scale standard. While most kitchen scales offer 1-gram resolution and slow response times, the Pearl S resolves to 0.1g with a 50-millisecond response — fast enough to track espresso shot weight in real time as it pours.

The 0.1g resolution matters most for espresso. Dialing in a recipe (e.g. 18g in, 36g out in 28 seconds) requires precision that drip-coffee scales can't deliver. The Pearl S handles this and the corresponding pour-over recipes equally well, with app integration that lets the user save and replay recipes across sessions.

The question is whether the premium over a $30-50 kitchen scale is justified. For pour-over brewing at the recipe-following level, a basic scale plus a stopwatch covers 90% of the use case. The Pearl S earns its price for users who tune recipes, weigh espresso shots in real-time, or want the smoothest workflow available — not for users who just need to measure beans.
