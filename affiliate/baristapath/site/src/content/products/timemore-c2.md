The Timemore Chestnut C2 is the canonical "first burr grinder under $100" recommendation for filter coffee. It pairs a 38mm steel conical burr set with an aluminum body, producing particle distributions that are dramatically better than blade grinders for a marginal price increase.

What buyers should expect: clean cups from pour-over at any common grind setting, and noticeably better consistency than the $30-50 blade grinder it replaces. What they shouldn't expect: the precision or consistency of a Comandante C40 at fine settings, or comfort during back-to-back grinding sessions.

The C2 is intentionally a stepping stone. Most users who fall into specialty coffee through a C2 upgrade within 18 months — either to an electric grinder (Baratza Encore or Fellow Opus) or to a premium hand grinder (Comandante C40, 1Zpresso JX). For its price tier, it's the right tool to start with.
