The Dedica EC685 solves one specific problem: you want an espresso machine but you have six inches of counter depth and a partner who notices new appliances. At under six inches wide, it slips between a microwave and a knife block.

The trade-offs are honest. The 51mm portafilter and pressurized baskets simplify the experience for someone who has never pulled a shot — coffee comes out under pressure even with a rough grind — but they also cap how far you can develop your technique with this exact machine.

We recommend the Dedica when space wins the argument over flexibility, or as a starter machine you fully expect to outgrow in 18 to 24 months. If neither applies, the Gaggia Classic Pro at a similar price tier is a better long-term choice.
