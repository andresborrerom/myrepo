The Espro Toroid is the upgrade pick when the budget Rattleware-class knock box runs out of capacity. Where the Rattleware Short holds roughly 12 pucks, the Toroid handles 20 — enough that a two-drink-a-day household empties it weekly instead of every three days.

The design difference is the toroid (donut) shape. A traditional cylindrical knock box absorbs the impact straight down through a vertical wall, which transmits force back into the counter and can rock the box. The Toroid spreads the impact around the curve, which keeps the box stable when the user knocks a puck out hard. The silicone non-slip base ring adds a second layer of stability that's missing on most budget alternatives.

The trade-off is footprint. At 5.5 inches tall and 7.5 inches wide, the Toroid sits on the counter — it does not slide under most espresso machine drip trays. For users with a Breville Barista Express, Rancilio Silvia, or Gaggia Classic Pro on a finished countertop, the height is fine and the extra capacity is worth the visual presence. For users hiding gear in a tight kitchen corner, the shorter Rattleware remains the smarter buy.
