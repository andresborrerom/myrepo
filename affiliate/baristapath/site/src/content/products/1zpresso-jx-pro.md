The 1Zpresso JX-Pro is the hand grinder that made espresso practical without electricity. Where the Comandante C40 reaches into espresso territory only with aftermarket modifications, the JX-Pro ships with a 200-click adjustment scale that gives genuine espresso dialing resolution.

The 48mm burr size is also notable. Most premium hand grinders use 38-40mm burrs to keep the unit compact; 1Zpresso uses the larger burr to speed up grinding and improve particle distribution at fine settings. The result is faster grinds and cleaner shots than smaller-burr competitors.

The trade-off is weight. The JX-Pro is meaningfully heavier than a Comandante or Timemore, which matters during long grinding sessions. For users grinding both espresso (where the click resolution wins) and filter (where the larger burr helps), the JX-Pro is the most practical "one hand grinder" pick under $200.
