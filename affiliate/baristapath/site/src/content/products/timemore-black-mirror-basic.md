The Timemore Black Mirror Basic is the answer to the question "do I need to spend $220 on an Acaia for V60 and AeroPress?" — and the answer is no. At ~$39 with 0.1g resolution, a built-in timer, and USB-C charging, the Basic covers every weighing job a pour-over or immersion brewer can throw at it.

What it does not do is real-time espresso shot weighing. The 500ms response time means the scale lags noticeably behind the cup as espresso pours into the basket — fine for weighing beans into a portafilter, too slow for live shot tracking against a recipe. For that workflow the Acaia Pearl S's 50ms response is the right tool. For pour-over, where pours happen over thirty-plus seconds and 0.1g of granularity matters more than millisecond reaction, the Basic is the right scale.

The honest caveats: there is no Bluetooth, no app, no flow rate display, and battery longevity is the typical Chinese-electronics story — excellent at year one, noticeably weaker by year three. Users who brew daily and replace their gear every five years should plan around eventually replacing it. For the price, the Basic is the AeroPress and V60 brewing scale we recommend by default — and most home brewers will never outgrow it.
