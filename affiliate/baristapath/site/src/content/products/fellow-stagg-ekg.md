The Fellow Stagg EKG is the most-recommended pour-over kettle in specialty coffee, and the recommendation is mostly justified by usability over raw specs. Variable temperature kettles in the $50-100 range often match the EKG on paper; what the EKG adds is the most refined pour spout in the category and a hold-temperature feature that maintains target temperature for an hour after boil.

For pour-over technique, the goose-neck shape matters more than most users expect. The Stagg's spout produces a consistent slow stream that lets the user control extraction during the bloom and main pour phases. Budget kettles with shorter or wider spouts pour faster and harder to control, which translates directly to less consistent cups.

The criticism is price. At $195 it's one of the most expensive kettles in the home coffee category. The Brewista Artisan or COSORI Pour-Over kettle offer 80% of the functionality for half the price. The Stagg buys design polish, a hold-temp feature competitors lack, and the smoothest pouring experience available. Whether that's worth the premium depends on how often pour-overs happen in the household.
