The Fellow Ode Gen 2 redefined the filter-only grinder category. With 64mm flat burrs in a sub-$400 grinder and a single-dose design with front-loading, it shipped specifications that previously lived in $700+ grinders.

The Gen 2 burr update was meaningful. The original Ode's burr geometry struggled with brighter light roasts, producing flat-tasting cups. Gen 2 addresses this with a redesigned cutting profile that handles the full filter range — from a fine pour-over to coarse French press — with consistent results.

The hard line is espresso. The Ode's burrs and stepped adjustment cannot reach the fineness or precision needed for espresso, and Fellow markets it explicitly as filter-only. For users wanting one grinder for both, look at the Niche Zero or the Sette 270. For filter-only households who value design, low retention, and large burrs at the price, the Ode is the clear winner.
