The AeroPress is one of the most universally recommended single-cup brewers in coffee, and the recommendation has held since 2005. The combination of immersion and pressure brewing produces cups that are cleaner than French press but with more body than pour-over — a unique flavor profile that's its own category.

For travelers and office workers, the AeroPress is unmatched. It packs into a small bag, requires only hot water and the brewer itself, and produces consistent cups regardless of grinder quality (within reason). The World AeroPress Championship has produced thousands of competition-winning recipes that home users can copy directly.

Limitations are around scale. The AeroPress brews one cup at a time, in roughly 90 seconds. For households brewing for two or more people simultaneously, a Chemex or Kalita Wave handles the workflow better. For single-cup users — and that's a large segment of specialty coffee — the AeroPress is hard to beat for $40.
