The OXO Brew Adjustable Temperature Gooseneck sits in the mid-tier slot between the $50 stovetop Hario Buono and the $195 Fellow Stagg EKG, and for most home users it is the better-value pick. It offers the same core features as the Stagg — 1-degree temperature control, hold-temp, gooseneck spout, built-in timer — at roughly half the price.

The differences vs the Stagg are real but small. Hold-temp lasts 30 minutes instead of 60, the build leans more on plastic than stainless steel, and the design is less photogenic for the open-shelf-coffee-bar aesthetic. The pour itself is competent — not as smooth as the Stagg's refined spout, but well within the range that produces clean V60 and Chemex cups. The integrated count-up timer is the genuinely useful detail: it starts on demand, sits next to the temperature readout, and removes the phone-timer juggling that the Stagg requires.

For daily brewers who want temperature control without the design-premium markup, the OXO is the honest recommendation. For users who brew light single-origin coffees and value the longer hold-temp window for between-brew adjustments, the Stagg's extra $95 is defensible. For everyone else, the OXO covers the job.
