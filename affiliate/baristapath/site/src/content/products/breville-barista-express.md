The Barista Express is Breville's longest-running consumer espresso platform and the model that defined "all-in-one" in the category. It combines a thermocoil brew system, a 54mm group, and a built-in conical burr grinder with stepped adjustment.

The integration has real benefits. You move one appliance, plug in one cord, clean one counter. The integrated grinder also dispenses straight into the portafilter, which keeps things tidy and reduces dose variance compared with weighing from a separate hopper.

The compromise is ceiling. The grinder is fine for milk drinks and forgiving roasts but will frustrate you with single-origin light roasts and very fine espresso adjustments. Many owners eventually pair the Barista Express with a standalone grinder and use only the brew side — at which point a Bambino Plus plus a real grinder would have been the better build. Buy this one if "one box" is the actual goal.
