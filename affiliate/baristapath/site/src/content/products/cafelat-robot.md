The Cafelat Robot is the simpler, less expensive cousin of the Flair 58. Both are manual lever machines with 58mm portafilters, but the Robot strips out the heated group element and pre-infusion chamber in favor of pure mechanical simplicity. Boil water, pour into chamber, lever down — that's the entire workflow.

What surprises new users is how good the shots can be. With a quality grinder and pre-heated portafilter, the Robot pulls espresso that competes with $1,500 prosumer machines for taste. The pressure gauge (on the "Robot Barista" version) makes it easy to learn pressure profiling — something most automatic machines hide entirely.

It's the wrong machine for milk drinks. Owners typically pair it with a stovetop frother or skip milk entirely. For users who drink mostly straight espresso and want commercial-grade extraction without commercial-grade investment, the Robot is one of the highest value-per-dollar machines in the market.
