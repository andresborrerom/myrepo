The OXO Brew Conical Burr Grinder is the most common "first burr grinder" recommendation from kitchen retailers — and the recommendation is reasonable. OXO is one of the few mainstream kitchen brands with a strong warranty and customer service operation, which matters for users who don't want to deal with specialty coffee equipment forums.

Specs-wise, it's a 40mm conical burr grinder with 38 stepped positions and a built-in timer. The timer is a useful upgrade over budget grinders that require holding a button — users can program 2-4 dose sizes and the grinder repeats them. Burr design and motor are middle-of-the-road for the price.

For filter coffee — drip, pour over, French press — the OXO Brew is a credible pick. It cannot match the Baratza Encore for parts availability or the Wilfa Svart for burr quality, but it wins on warranty and retail accessibility. For households who want to upgrade from a blade grinder without joining the specialty coffee community, this is the right product.
