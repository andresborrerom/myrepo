The Profitec Pro 300 is the compact dual-boiler answer for prosumer buyers who want true simultaneous brew-and-steam capability without an E61 group's footprint and warm-up time. Two independent PID-controlled boilers, both adjustable from the front panel, give it more flexibility than most single-boiler-with-PID machines at the same price.

The 58mm portafilter alone justifies attention. Anyone moving up from a 54mm Breville or 57mm Lelit gains compatibility with the broader commercial accessory market — VST baskets, bottomless portafilters, leveling distributors. The Pro 300's quick-heating brew boiler also means it's ready in under 15 minutes from cold, faster than an E61 by a meaningful margin.

What it sacrifices versus the Pro 600 or 700 line: rotary pump, larger steam boiler, plumb-in capability. For a single-household espresso bar pulling 4-6 drinks per session, those omissions don't show. For a heavier user or someone who wants to plumb directly to a water line, step up the range.
