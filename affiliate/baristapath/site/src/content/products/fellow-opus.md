The Fellow Opus extends the Ode's single-dose design philosophy into the all-purpose space. Where the Ode is filter-only with 64mm flat burrs, the Opus uses 40mm conical burrs with an espresso range built in — a different cost optimization for users who want one grinder for both methods.

For under $200, the Opus is the most modern-looking option in the all-purpose category. The single-dose front-loader, anti-static treatment, and 41 stepped positions give it real espresso capability — not at the level of a Sette 270 or Eureka, but functional for users running a Bambino or Gaggia Classic.

Where it doesn't compete: against dedicated espresso grinders at twice the price (Niche, Eureka Specialita) for espresso consistency, and against the Ode for filter coffee quality. The Opus is a versatile compromise — and at the price, that compromise is genuinely competitive.
