The De'Longhi La Specialista is De'Longhi's answer to the Breville Barista Express: an all-in-one espresso machine with an integrated conical burr grinder, designed for users who want one box instead of two on the counter. It's pitched at the intermediate user who has graduated past pod machines but isn't building a dedicated coffee station.

The active dual-heater system is the technical pitch. Rather than a single boiler doing both brew and steam, La Specialista uses separate heaters that can stabilize independently. It's not a true dual-boiler (no PID, no commercial group), but in real use it delivers more consistent shots than a single-thermoblock machine.

The 51mm portafilter is the strategic compromise. It's a De'Longhi-proprietary size, which means almost no aftermarket basket or bottomless portafilter options. For users who plan to mod and upgrade, this is a blocker — and they should look at the Barista Express (54mm Breville ecosystem) or step up to a separate 58mm machine and dedicated grinder.
