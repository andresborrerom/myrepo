The Kalita Wave is the forgiving alternative to the V60 in the pour-over category. Its flat-bottom design with three small drainage holes regulates flow more consistently than a V60's single large hole — meaning small pour-technique mistakes produce smaller flavor differences in the cup.

For beginners learning pour-over, the Wave produces drinkable coffee on the first try in ways the V60 doesn't. Channeling (water finding the path of least resistance through the bed) is reduced by the flat bottom, and the corrugated wave-shaped filter prevents the paper from collapsing against the brewer walls.

The stainless steel 185 model is the most travel-practical option in the Wave family. It survives drops, packs flat, and doesn't have the temperature instability of metal at scale. Filters are slightly less ubiquitous than V60 paper in mainstream retail — most specialty coffee shops carry both, but supermarkets typically don't.
