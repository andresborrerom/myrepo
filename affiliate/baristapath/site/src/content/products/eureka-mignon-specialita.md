The Eureka Mignon Specialita is one of the most recommended sub-$1,000 espresso grinders on the market, and the reason is unromantic: it is quiet, consistent, and built well enough to disappear into a daily routine for a decade.

The 55mm flat burrs produce more uniform particle distributions than conical alternatives at the same price, which generally translates to slightly cleaner espresso flavor profiles. Stepless adjustment with an LCD timer makes dialing precise and repeatable — turn the collar a hair, time the next shot, settle in.

The Specialita is purpose-built for espresso. Coarser grinds for pour-over or French press are technically possible but inconsistent. Households with multiple brew methods should consider the broader-range Eureka Atom or step horizontally to a Sette 270 (espresso) plus an Encore (filter) combination at similar total cost.
