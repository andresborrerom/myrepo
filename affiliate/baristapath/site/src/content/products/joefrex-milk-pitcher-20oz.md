Joe Frex is a European coffee-tools brand that competes with Rattleware on the basics and undercuts most premium pitchers on price. The 20oz model is the pitcher that fills the gap in most home kitchens — too small for a single 6oz cappuccino, too large to waste milk steaming for two drinks at once.

The pour spout is the design choice that distinguishes Joe Frex from cheaper 20oz competitors. The spout is cut narrower than the Bellman's wider equivalent, which produces a tighter and more controllable stream during the pour. For users committed to learning latte art at the multi-drink batch size, the Joe Frex spout is closer to the 12oz competition pitcher experience than most 20oz pitchers manage.

The omission is the thermometer mount. Where the Bellman 20oz includes a pre-drilled hole for a clip-on dial thermometer, the Joe Frex leaves the side smooth. For users learning microfoam by touch, this is fine; for users who want a visible temperature reference during the first weeks of practice, the Bellman wins on this specific feature. At $10 less than the Bellman, the Joe Frex is the right pick for users who already know when milk hits 60-65C by hand-feel or who use a separate probe thermometer.
