The Casabrews 3700 Essential is one of the most credible options in the under-$150 espresso category. It marks 20-bar pump pressure on the spec sheet (the actual brew pressure is regulated lower by an OPV), includes a steam wand for milk, and ships with both pressurized and non-pressurized baskets.

For the price, the build is surprisingly solid: stainless steel exterior, reasonable thermoblock warm-up, and a functional steam wand. The pressurized basket is what most users will rely on — it forgives bad grind and tamping by mechanically pressurizing the puck. Non-pressurized baskets are included but require a real burr grinder to perform.

It will not produce shots that compete with $500+ machines, and the proprietary portafilter caps upgrade potential. As an entry point for someone curious about home espresso who isn't ready to commit $300-500, it does the job — and pairs well with a sub-$150 manual grinder for first-year exploration.
