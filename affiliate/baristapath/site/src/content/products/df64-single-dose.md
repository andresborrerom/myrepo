The DF64 is the grinder that made 64mm flat burrs accessible at under $500. It originated as a rebadge of various Chinese factory grinders sold under different brand names — DF64, MiiCoffee, Solo, Turin — all sharing the same chassis and core mechanism.

For espresso-and-filter households who want flat-burr extraction quality without paying Eureka or Niche prices, the DF64 is the practical answer. Stepless adjustment handles the full range, single-dosing keeps retention to half a gram, and the front-loader design is genuinely convenient.

The friction is quality control. Some units arrive with burrs not aligned to spec and need user adjustment. The modding community treats this as a feature, not a bug — burr upgrades (SSP, Mazzer, etc.), motor swaps, and bellows mods are all common. For users who want a turnkey "open the box and grind" experience, the Eureka Mignon is the more polished pick at a similar price.
