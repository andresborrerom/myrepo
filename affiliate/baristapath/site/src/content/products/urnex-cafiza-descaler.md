Urnex Cafiza is the standard espresso-machine cleaning powder in specialty coffee, both at home and in commercial cafes. The 566g jar lasts the typical home user a full year of weekly backflushing, which is the maintenance cadence most service techs recommend for a Breville Dual Boiler, Gaggia Classic Pro, or any prosumer machine with a three-way solenoid.

The most common confusion: Cafiza is a backflush cleaner, not a descaler. The alkaline formula breaks down coffee oils and rancid residue inside the group head, gaskets, and brew lines. It does not remove limescale (calcium deposits) from boilers — that is the job of a citric or lactic acid descaler. Most machines need both products on different schedules: Cafiza weekly for coffee oils, descaler every 3-6 months for scale (interval depends on water hardness).

The practical limitation is the blind basket. Cafiza is dosed into a blind portafilter basket (no holes) which is then flushed through the machine. Most prosumer machines either ship with one or sell one as a $15 accessory. Super-automatic machines (De'Longhi Magnifica, Breville Oracle Touch) generally lack a manual backflush path entirely — those machines use proprietary cleaning tablets dropped into a dedicated chamber instead. For semi-auto owners, Cafiza is the universal answer.
