The Rattleware 12oz pitcher is the default latte-art pitcher in most specialty coffee shops, and it earns the same default position at home. The 12oz size is calibrated for a single 6-8oz milk drink — fill it with cold milk to just below the spout base, steam to roughly double volume, and you end up at the right pour temperature with the right amount of milk for a 6oz cappuccino or 8oz latte.

The pour spout is the reason coffee shops settled on this pitcher over the years. The cut is sharp and narrow, which produces a controllable thin stream for drawing rosetta lines or a fast wide pour for tulip stacks. Cheaper pitchers with rounded spouts produce a soft, fat pour that's much harder to control during the final detail phase of latte art.

The trade-offs are basic. There's no insulation, so the pitcher gets hot enough to need a towel during longer steaming sessions. The thin metal dents easily, particularly the spout edge. For one-drink-a-day home use these aren't real problems. For a household making milk drinks for guests or batching multiple drinks, step up to a 20oz pitcher and consider one with thermometer mount holes.
