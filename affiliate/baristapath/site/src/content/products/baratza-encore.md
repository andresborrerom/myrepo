The Baratza Encore is the most-recommended entry-level burr grinder for filter coffee in North America, and the recommendation has held up for over a decade. The reasons are practical: it grinds consistently in the filter range, parts are available through Baratza's US service network, and replacement burrs cost a fraction of the machine.

What the Encore is not is an espresso grinder. The 40-step adjustment doesn't go fine enough to dial espresso reliably, and at the finest settings, particle distribution gets uneven. For espresso, look at the Encore ESP (a related model with espresso-range burrs) or move up to a Sette 270, Eureka Mignon, or similar dedicated espresso grinder.

For pour over, drip, French press, and cold brew, the Encore is hard to beat at the price. Its longevity also matters — Baratza supports parts and repair on units that are 8-10 years old, which is unusual for the consumer grinder category and changes the long-term cost calculation.
