The Eureka Mignon Silenzio is the quieter, simpler version of the Specialita. Same 50mm flat-burr platform, same Italian construction, same stepless adjustment — but without the LCD timer and with extra acoustic dampening that makes it the quietest electric espresso grinder under $700.

For users who grind espresso in the early morning while housemates sleep, the noise difference matters. The Silenzio is roughly 10 dB quieter than budget alternatives at the burr, and Eureka's motor mount design absorbs vibration that would otherwise travel through the chassis to the counter.

The trade-off versus the Specialita is the timer. Specialita users can program shot weights by time; Silenzio users grind on-demand and stop manually. For single-dose workflows where weight is measured before grinding, the timer is redundant — saving $150 on the Silenzio is a legitimate optimization.
