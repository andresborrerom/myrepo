The Baratza Encore ESP is Baratza's response to a long-standing complaint: the original Encore couldn't dial espresso. The ESP keeps the same chassis and motor but redesigns the burr geometry and adjustment to give 20 dedicated espresso steps below the filter range.

In practice, the Encore ESP is the cheapest grinder that can dial a Breville Bambino or Gaggia Classic without obvious frustration. Shot consistency improves meaningfully over the standard Encore, and the espresso range stays usable across roast levels. It will not match a Eureka Mignon Specialita or a Niche Zero — those grinders use larger or differently-shaped burrs that produce more even particle distributions — but at $200, it's the value pick.

For users with a single brew method (only espresso or only pour-over), there are better dedicated grinders at the same price. For households making both espresso and filter from the same grinder, the Encore ESP is the sensible compromise.
