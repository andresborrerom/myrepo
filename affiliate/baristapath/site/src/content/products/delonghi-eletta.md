The De'Longhi Eletta Explore is one of the most feature-loaded super-automatics on the consumer market. Beyond standard espresso and milk drinks, it includes a true cold extraction mode that produces cold-brew-style coffee in roughly five minutes — a feature competitive super-autos in this tier don't offer.

The LatteCrema system is the practical headliner. A separate milk carafe stores in the fridge between sessions and snaps onto the machine for one-touch latte or cappuccino. Quality is consistent across drinks, which matters for households where multiple users want a hands-off experience.

The pricing is the friction point. At ~$1,500 you're approaching prosumer semi-automatic territory, where a Profitec Pro 300 plus a Eureka Mignon Specialita would deliver objectively better espresso. The Eletta sells against that comparison on convenience: cold brew, scheduled drinks, milk automation, and zero learning curve.
