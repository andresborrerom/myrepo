The Wilfa Svart Aroma is a quietly excellent filter grinder that punches above its price class. The headline spec is the 58mm conical burr set — burrs that size typically live in $400+ grinders — and the stepless adjustment makes it competitive with grinders twice its price for pour-over use.

The product was developed in collaboration with Tim Wendelboe, the Norwegian World Brewers Cup champion. The dial calibration and adjustment range reflect a specialty filter coffee perspective: light roasts dial in cleanly, particle distribution stays even, and the unit handles bright single-origin beans without the muddiness budget grinders introduce.

Espresso is the hard line. The Svart Aroma cannot reach espresso fineness, and Wilfa markets it as a filter grinder explicitly. For pure filter households who want premium burrs without paying premium prices, the Svart is one of the most undervalued picks in the segment — when stock is available in the US.
