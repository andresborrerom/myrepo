The Rocket Appartamento is the design-forward choice in the HX prosumer category. Italian-made with a copper boiler, polished stainless chassis, and the full E61 group, it sits visually in a different class from German competitors at the same price.

Functionally it's a traditional HX machine: simultaneous brew and steam, E61 thermal mass, vibratory pump. The catch — and it's a real one — is the absence of PID. Brew temperature is managed by a thermostat and the user's flush routine: a short flush before each shot cools the brew water to the right zone. Skilled users handle this routinely; new prosumer buyers should know it's a daily ritual.

For users who prioritize how a machine looks on the counter and want commercial-grade espresso, the Appartamento delivers. Users who want PID stability without manual flushing should look at the Lelit Mara X or ECM Synchronika at adjacent price points.
