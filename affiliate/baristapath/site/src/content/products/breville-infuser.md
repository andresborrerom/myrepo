The Breville Infuser predates the Bambino Plus and Barista Touch in the Breville lineup, but it remains a strong pick for users who want PID and a manual steam wand without an integrated grinder. Its low-pressure preinfusion ramp is more pronounced than the auto-machines in the family, which favors slightly lighter roasts that benefit from a gentler bloom.

What separates it from the Bambino is the manual steam wand and full programmability. Where Bambino automates milk for ease, the Infuser hands the wand back to the user — closer to the workflow you'd get on a Gaggia Classic Pro but with PID stability and programmable shot volumes layered on top.

It's an older model in the Breville lineup, so resale and parts availability are weaker than the current Barista Pro/Touch generation. Buy it new if you want manual steam plus PID at this price; for resale value over time, the Barista Pro is the safer pick.
