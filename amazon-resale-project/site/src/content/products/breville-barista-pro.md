The Barista Pro sits one step above the Barista Express in Breville's lineup. The main upgrades are the Thermojet heating system (3-second warm-up vs about 60 seconds) and the LCD interface, which surfaces the shot timer and grind selection more clearly.

If you already own the Barista Express, the upgrade math is honest: the Pro is faster and prettier, but the brewing fundamentals are similar. The integrated grinder is still the ceiling.

We recommend the Barista Pro over the Express only if (a) fast warm-up matters to your daily flow, or (b) you want the more modern interface. Otherwise the Express delivers similar shots for less money, and a Bambino Plus plus a dedicated grinder at the same combined price extracts better.
