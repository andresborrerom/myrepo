The Mahlkonig X54 brings the burr geometry and engineering from Mahlkonig's commercial grinders (EK43, E80) into a consumer-priced home grinder. The X54 is not a downsized EK — but the burr design philosophy and the company's reputation for grinding precision flow through.

What you get for the price: 54mm flat burrs with Mahlkonig's particle distribution characteristics, stepless macro and micro adjustment, and a build quality that approaches commercial. The grinder handles espresso through drip without drama, and the German parts ecosystem means long-term serviceability.

What you don't get versus single-dose competitors: the lowest retention numbers. The X54 is designed as a hopper-fed grinder; users can single-dose, but retention sits higher than purpose-built single-dose machines (Niche, DF64). For users who keep one bean rotation and value Mahlkonig's commercial DNA, the X54 is a top-tier home grinder.
