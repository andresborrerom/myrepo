The Baratza Sette 270 is one of the most genuinely innovative consumer espresso grinders. Its design rotates the outer burr (rather than the inner one) and feeds grounds directly through the bottom of the burr chamber into the portafilter — eliminating the retention chamber that most grinders suffer from.

For users who single-dose (weigh out beans per shot rather than keeping a hopper full), the Sette 270 retains less than 0.5g shot-to-shot, which is competitive with $1,000 single-dose grinders. The stepless micro-adjustment lets dialing happen in 9 micro-clicks between 30 macro positions, giving the resolution needed for espresso.

What you give up is noise and longevity. The Sette is loud — meaningfully louder than a Eureka Mignon — and the chassis is plastic where competitors use metal. For users prioritizing low retention and fast grind speed over build quality, the Sette is the right pick. For users who want a grinder that will run quietly for 15 years, look at the Eureka Mignon Specialita.
