The Hario V60-02 is the most-used pour-over dripper in specialty coffee. Its 60-degree cone, spiral interior ribs, and large bottom hole give the brewer control over extraction in a way Kalita Wave or flat-bottom alternatives don't — at the cost of a steeper learning curve.

The ceramic version is the recommended choice for home use. It retains heat better than plastic, which matters across a 3-4 minute brew time, and it doesn't transfer flavor like uncoated metal can on light roasts. The size 02 handles 1-4 cups in a single brew, which covers most household scenarios.

What the V60 requires that other brewers don't: a goose-neck kettle for pouring (the spiraling pour technique is core to V60 method), a quality burr grinder (the V60 reveals grinder weaknesses other methods hide), and practice. Users who don't have those investments should consider an AeroPress or Chemex first; users who do will find the V60 rewards skill at a level few brewers match.
