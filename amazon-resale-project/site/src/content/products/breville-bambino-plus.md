The Breville Bambino Plus targets the home barista who wants near-instant warm-up and assisted milk texturing without stepping up to a dual boiler. The Thermojet thermocoil reaches brew temperature in about three seconds, which is the headline difference versus a traditional thermoblock machine.

Its automatic steam wand draws milk to a temperature and froth setting you choose, then shuts off — handy if you are still building muscle memory on manual texturing. Serious users typically replace the stock pressurized baskets with non-pressurized ones to unlock real extraction control, and pair the machine with a quality burr grinder.

Footprint matters at this price: at roughly 7.5 inches wide, the Bambino Plus fits in apartment kitchens where a Linea Mini or Profitec would not. Compromises live in the 54mm portafilter (limits aftermarket parts) and the plastic-heavy chassis, but the brewing fundamentals — 9-bar pump, preinfusion, PID — are solid for the segment.
