The Niche Zero is the grinder that popularized single-dosing in the consumer market. Built around 63mm Mazzer Kony conical burrs (the same family used in commercial grinders), the Niche is designed around the assumption that the user will weigh out beans per shot rather than keep a hopper full.

The retention number — under 0.1g shot-to-shot — is the whole product. Users who rotate frequently between bean origins or roast levels don't lose flavor crossover from one batch to the next. The stepless adjustment ring covers everything from Turkish-fine to French-press-coarse, making it one of the few grinders that genuinely handles all brew methods without compromise.

The friction is availability. Niche is a small British operation, and US units historically ship via direct order with waitlist periods that can stretch weeks or months. For users who can wait and want a single grinder that handles espresso and filter both, the Niche is the canonical recommendation in the under-$1,000 single-dose category.
