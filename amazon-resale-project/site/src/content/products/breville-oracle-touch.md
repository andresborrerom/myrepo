The Oracle Touch is Breville's flagship and one of the few machines that splits the difference between a super-automatic and a semi-automatic. You get an integrated grinder, automatic tamping, dual boilers, and a touchscreen with one-button drinks — but the portafilter still detaches, runs at 58mm, and the milk wand is real steam.

This makes it the answer to a specific question: how do I get cafe-quality espresso without becoming a barista? The Oracle Touch answers honestly. Calibration takes some setup, but day-to-day use is a few button presses.

The premium over the Dual Boiler buys the automation, not better espresso fundamentals. If you enjoy the craft of pulling shots, the Dual Boiler or a Linea Mini will reward you more per dollar. If you want a reliable morning routine for a household, the Oracle Touch earns its price.
